Privacy Policy

Slaydoku by Clayver Games • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Clayver Games ("we", "our", or "us") built Slaydoku (package ID com.slaydoku.pups) as a cozy mobile logic puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored, how third-party services operate, and how you control your data.

1. Local Device Storage

Your game progress—including campaign level, streaks, coins, unlocked pup breeds/avatars/frames, your chosen display name, numeric Player ID, and accessibility/theme preferences—is stored locally on your device. We do not require account registration, email addresses, passwords, or phone numbers to play Slaydoku.

2. Optional Play With Friends (Firebase Realtime Database)

When you choose to host or join an online Play With Friends room, Slaydoku exchanges temporary room state (6-character room code, chosen display name, board size, and live match progress/finish time) through Google Firebase Realtime Database strictly to synchronize the active match between you and your friend. Room records are ephemeral and contain no sensitive personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Slaydoku uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads (for extra rewards or retries) and occasional interstitial ads between solo levels (unless you own the No-Ads Pack). Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and diagnostics for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and—where permitted by your regional consent settings—personalized advertising.

  • Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • You can reset or limit your Android Advertising ID at any time in your Android device under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Slaydoku is designed for a general puzzle audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your region). All ad requests are configured with a General (G) maximum ad content rating.

5. Data Deletion & Control

Because your game save lives locally on your device, you can permanently delete all Slaydoku save data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android (Settings → Apps → Slaydoku → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Clayver Games at: clayvergames.support@gmail.com.
