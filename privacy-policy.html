Privacy Policy

Slaydoku by Clayver Games • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Clayver Games ("we", "our", or "us") built Slaydoku (Android package ID com.slaydoku.pups) as a cozy mobile logic puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored on your device, how third-party services operate, and how you can manage or delete your data.

1. Local Device Storage

Your game progress—including campaign levels, streaks, coins, gems, unlocked pup breeds, avatars, frames, your chosen display name, numeric Player ID, and accessibility or theme preferences—is stored strictly locally on your device using browser localStorage and native Android Preferences. We do not require account registration, email addresses, passwords, or phone numbers to play Slaydoku.

2. Play With Friends (Google Firebase Realtime Database)

When you choose to host or join an online Play With Friends room, Slaydoku exchanges temporary match state—consisting of a 6-character room code, chosen display name, board size, and live match progress and solution proof—through Google Firebase Realtime Database strictly to synchronize the active match between you and your friend. All network traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS, room records contain no personal contact information, and rooms are cleared or overwritten after matches end.

  • Firebase Privacy & Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Slaydoku uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads (for optional bonus rewards or retries) and occasional interstitial ads between solo levels (which may be reduced or removed by optional ad-free features, where available). Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process the following data categories for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and—where permitted by your consent settings—personalized advertising:

  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • App interactions (such as ad impressions, views, and taps)
  • Diagnostics (crash logs and performance telemetry)
  • Device or other IDs (including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID)
  • Google Advertising Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • In-App Consent Management: Where required by regional privacy laws (such as EEA/UK GDPR or applicable US state privacy laws), you can review, manage, or withdraw your ad consent at any time inside Slaydoku under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Device-Level Ad Controls: You can reset or limit your Android Advertising ID at any time in your Android device under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Slaydoku is intended for a general puzzle audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All ad requests are configured with a General (G) maximum ad content rating. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information, please contact us at clayvergames.support@gmail.com.

5. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

  • Security in Transit: All connections to Firebase Realtime Database and Google AdMob are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local Save Retention & Deletion: Your local game save stays on your device until you erase it. You can permanently delete all local Slaydoku save data at any time using in-app Settings → Reset Local Data, via Android (Settings → Apps → Slaydoku → Storage → Clear data), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Ephemeral Rooms & AdMob Retention: Online Play With Friends room entries are ephemeral and cleared or overwritten after matches end. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained and managed in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

6. Regional Rights, Grievance Contact & Policy Changes

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK under GDPR, US states with consumer privacy laws, and India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act / DPDP), you may have rights to access, delete, or withdraw consent regarding your data.

Privacy & Grievance Contact: Clayver Games — clayvergames.support@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy: We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Slaydoku evolves. Any updates will be published at this URL with an updated Effective Date.
